# how-organic-is-x402
*Analyzing organic vs. gamed transaction volume on the x402 agentic payments protocol*


x402 is an open-source payments protocol that lets AI agents pay for API calls with crypto micropayments. It's seen $108M in volume since launch in May 2025, though the quality of the data has been an area of debate. Artemis Analytics provides a comprehensive dataset distinguishing organic vs gamed x402 volume. The purpose of this analysis is to dive deeper into the data to answer a simple question: just how organic is x402? 

## Findings

**39% of total volume ($42M) shows gamed signals.** That number is real but misleading. The median day sits at just 10.2% gamed. A handful of extreme days drag the mean to 24.3%.

**Gaming is extremely concentrated.** 50% of all gamed volume came from 12 days. 80% came from 42. This isn't a persistent problem across the protocol. It's a spike.

**The spike was Oct-Dec 2025.** Millions of sub-$1 transactions, peaking at 59% average gamed in November. Classic wash trading / incentive farming pattern that activated during a period of high attention on the protocol.

**The current state of x402 looks healthy.** March 2026 onward: all-time high volume ($1.9M/day avg), 17% gamed, and average transaction sizes in the $15-40 range. Structurally different from the gaming era.


## Data

Artemis Analytics x402 Data May 2025 to April 2026, 336 active days.
